Privacy Policy

AI Handoff is used only by its owner, for personal backup between the owner's own computers.

Google user data

The app accesses Google Drive only to store and retrieve the owner's own encrypted backup files. That data stays on the owner's computers and in the owner's own Google Drive.

No data is collected by, or sent to, any server operated by this app. Google user data is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party, and it is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
